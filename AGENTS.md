# Storefront direction

The storefront is intended to become an app and a mobile app. Treat mobile shopping as a first-class requirement for future changes.

- Check layouts at 320, 390, and 430 pixels wide as well as desktop. Prevent page-wide horizontal scrolling; keep intentional card carousels swipeable.
- Keep navigation, product choices, cart, and checkout usable with touch, keyboard, and screen readers. Aim for at least 44-pixel touch targets for primary controls.
- Account for phone safe areas, dynamic viewport height, and the fixed bottom navigation. Do not cover important content or checkout actions.
- Preserve the homepage order requested by the user: the journal follows “Organic Farm Moments On Instagram”.
- Keep shared shopping components reusable for future app work. Do not claim native app readiness based only on responsive browser checks.
